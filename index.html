About

I started as a software engineer, spending 2+ years at SAP Labs building conversational AI for Joule, before the generative-AI wave. After an MBA at IIM Kozhikode, I moved into product: at Walmart I worked in the Marketplace pillar on seller fraud risk detection, and today at Expedia Group I'm in Lodging Connectivity Supply, building AI-forward developer tools that speed up partner adoption and bring inventory to Expedia and Vrbo at scale.

I care about the parts of an AI product that decide whether people can trust it: a sharp problem, a clear quality bar, guardrails that hold, and an experience that shows its sources. Here I share what I build and what I learn, including what didn't work. Based in Seattle.
